Welcome to Tomo

The capital formation operating system.

Tomo isn't a CRM replacement. It's the operational layer that does the work alongside your existing pipeline tools — drafting outreach, surfacing drift, executing the disciplined patterns that move a raise forward.

A note on what Tomo sees and doesn't. Tomo only flags drift when email, calendar, and CRM all show no activity — so an LP you've been calling won't get flagged as "quiet" just because you haven't emailed. If you do see a flag that's wrong, the Relationships page has an "Actually I'm in touch (off-channel)" affordance — Tomo learns from your correction.
